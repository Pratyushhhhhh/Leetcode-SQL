Input: 
Employee table:
+-----+-------+------------+-----------+
| id  | name  | department | managerId |
+-----+-------+------------+-----------+
| 101 | John  | A          | null      |
| 102 | Dan   | A          | 101       |
| 103 | James | A          | 101       |
| 104 | Amy   | A          | 101       |
| 105 | Anne  | A          | 101       |
| 106 | Ron   | B          | 101       |
+-----+-------+------------+-----------+
Output: 
+------+
| name |
+------+
| John |
+------+

# Write your MySQL query statement below
SELECT e1.name
FROM Employee e1
INNER JOIN Employee e2
ON e1.id = e2.managerId
GROUP BY e2.managerID
HAVING COUNT(e2.managerId) >= 5
